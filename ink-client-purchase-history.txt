SEASONAL ASSUMPTIONS
--------------------
Peak printing season: October–March
Active season: September–April
Off-season: May–August

The sample history is designed to show more frequent and generally larger
replenishment orders during the peak season, with lower purchasing activity
during the off-season. Orders usually contain one or two colours; complete
CMYK orders are uncommon.

FORECASTING NOTES
-----------------
1. Peak season is October–March; active season is September–April.
2. Off-season is May–August, with lower purchasing activity.
3. Orders are irregular and generally replenish one or two colours.
4. Full CMYK replenishment orders are intentionally uncommon.
5. This synthetic sample has higher cumulative Black and Cyan purchases.
6. Purchases are replenishment events, not direct measurements of ink consumption.
7. Forecast accuracy should eventually be checked against stock balances or
   actual consumption records, where available.
